## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, minimalist aesthetic rooted in calm elegance and effortless clarity. Designed for modern lifestyle, creative productivity, and wellness software, the visual atmosphere balances serene lightness with purposeful structure. 

The aesthetic marries pure minimalism with tactile, ultra-soft depth. Surfaces feel light, airy, and inviting rather than sterile, achieved through tonal lavender-slate backgrounds, seamless borderless containers, and deeply diffused atmospheric shadows. The emotional tone evokes focus, gentle sophistication, and intuitive ease.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by soft violet tones, airy lavender washes, and balanced lilac slates, accented by purposeful deep violet and warm coral-purple validation cues.

- **Primary (`#7C5CFC`)**: Soft royal violet. Used for primary calls-to-action, key focus highlights, and active state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#B8A7EA`)**: Muted lilac mist. Used for tonal pill backgrounds, subtle tags, hover fills, and decorative gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#F08C7D`)**: Gentle coral-purple. Reserved strictly for non-aggressive warning states, urgent badges, and soft destructive highlights.
- **Neutral (`#6E6A7C`)**: Lilac slate. Sets body copy, secondary metadata, and icon fills without introducing harsh achromatic blacks or cold grays.

### Functional Foundations
- **Canvas Base**: `#F9F8FD` — a warm, luminous lavender-tinted white.
- **Surface Level 1**: `#FFFFFF` — clean card and container planes that lift gently off the tinted canvas.
- **Surface Level 2**: `#F3F0FA` — recessed wells, input backgrounds, and segmented track controls.
- **Text Primary**: `#252033` — deep aubergine-tinted black ensuring WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Text Secondary**: `#6E6A7C` — mid lilac-slate for supporting microcopy and placeholders.
- **Text Subdued**: `#9F9AA8` — disabled states and inactive watermarks.
- **State Notice / Warning**: Background `#FFF3EE`, text `#C85A48` for a gentle, human-centered feedback loop devoid of harsh alert reds.

## Typography

The typography uses **Montserrat** exclusively across all scales to ensure stylistic cohesion, balancing its geometric structure with high personality.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Montserrat Bold (weight 700) delivers confident, modern anchors with slightly negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain tightness and graphic impact.
- **Body Text**: Montserrat Regular (weight 400) provides open geometric counters and relaxed line heights for effortless scanning across articles, dashboards, and feeds.
- **Form Labels & Microcopy**: Montserrat Semi-Bold/Regular (weights 600 and 400) are calibrated with open tracking for crisp readability at small scale beneath input fields.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a relaxed, centered 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content canvas of 1280px, avoiding visual overcrowding through intentional horizontal and vertical breathing room.

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column grid, `margin` of 3rem (48px), and `gutter` of 1.5rem (24px). Containers prioritize generous vertical whitespace (`space-xl`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid, `margin` of 2rem (32px), `gutter` of 1.25rem (20px). Multi-column cards collapse gracefully to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout, `margin-sm` of 1.25rem (20px), and `gutter-sm` of 1rem (16px). Cards span the full safe width.

Internal component rhythm operates on a strict 8px-based baseline (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`), preventing cramped interfaces and letting the borderless components demarcate spatial zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal, atmospheric shadows tinted with lavender and deep violet rather than neutral black or harsh borders. Surfaces remain 100% borderless, floating softly over the light lilac-tinted canvas.

- **Elevation 0 (Canvas Base)**: Flat `#F9F8FD`, no shadow.
- **Elevation 1 (Cards, Modules, Sidebars)**: `#FFFFFF` surface lifted by an ambient, diffused shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(118, 92, 198, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(118, 92, 198, 0.03);`
- **Elevation 2 (Floating Dropdowns, Popovers, Active Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` surface elevated with an extended blur:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -6px rgba(103, 76, 186, 0.12), 0 6px 18px -3px rgba(103, 76, 186, 0.05);`
- **Interactive Button Elevation**: Primary buttons use a soft, glow-diffused violet drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -3px rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.35);`
  On active press, the shadow contracts to `0 4px 10px -2px rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.25)`.
- **Recessed Inset (Form Inputs, Track Wells)**: Borderless plane using filled color `#F3F0FA` with an inner shadow:
  `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px 0 rgba(76, 62, 105, 0.04);`

## Shapes

The design system embraces a pronounced, rounded shape geometry that softens the UI and complements the borderless aesthetic. 

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Styled using `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px), creating smooth, pebble-like surfaces that define layout sections without borders.
- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons**: Built using `rounded-2xl` (16px to 20px) or full pills (`9999px`) for badges and primary action buttons.
- **Micro Elements**: Checkboxes, icon badges, and tooltips utilize a coordinated `rounded-xl` (12px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#7C5CFC` background, text `#FFFFFF` in Montserrat Semi-Bold. Border-radius is pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Elevated with a soft violet diffused drop shadow (`rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.35)`). Zero border.
- **Secondary**: Soft tone-on-tone background `#F1ECFE`, text `#7C5CFC`. No outline. On hover, background transitions smoothly to `#E8E0FD`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#6E6A7C`. On hover, background fills with `#F3F0FA` with zero movement.

### Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Structure**: Borderless, filled background (`#F3F0FA`), `rounded-2xl`, height 52px, horizontal padding 20px. Placeholder text in `#9F9AA8`.
- **Focus State**: Background shifts to `#FFFFFF` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 92, 252, 0.18), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(118, 92, 198, 0.08)`. No solid strokes.
- **Labels**: Placed above the input field, styled in Montserrat Regular (`13px`, `#6E6A7C`), tracking slight positive.
- **Validation & Helper Text**: Always positioned 6px directly below the input field in Montserrat Regular (`12px`).
  - *Standard Helper*: `#6E6A7C`.
  - *Warning State*: Input background becomes `#FFF8F6`. Helper microcopy renders in `#C85A48` accompanied by a soft, gentle coral-purple pill chip.

### Warning Badges & Chips
- **Validation Badges**: Subtle rounded-full pills, zero border. Background `#FFF2EE`, text `#C85A48`, Montserrat Semi-Bold `11px`. Used to flag non-blocking corrections gently without visual alarm.
- **Filter Chips**: Inactive state has `#F3F0FA` fill and `#6E6A7C` text. Selected state has solid `#7C5CFC` with `#FFFFFF` text and a subtle diffused shadow.

### Cards & Containers
- Clean, completely borderless surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with generous internal padding (`32px` to `40px`) and `rounded-3xl` corners.
- Elevation is driven solely by the dual-stage ambient violet shadow described in Elevation & Depth.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 22px × 22px borderless boxes with `rounded-lg` (8px for checkbox, full pill for radio). Unselected state utilizes background `#EDE8F7`. Checked state fills with `#7C5CFC` featuring a crisp white SVG check or dot, surrounded by a soft violet glow.

### Lists & Tables
- Borderless table styling with alternating rows or generous row spacing (`16px` vertical gaps between distinct floating card rows).
- Section headers set in Montserrat Bold (`12px`, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`, text `#9F9AA8`).
